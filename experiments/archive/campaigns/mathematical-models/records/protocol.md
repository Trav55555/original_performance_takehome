## Oracles and kill rules

Frozen execution + independent reference + unchanged memory + lane/capacity validation are strong for tested executions. Exact identities are algebraic evidence. Capacity counts, dependency releases, and toy wins are diagnostics, never complete execution evidence. Unknown is unknown; in this program no solver is invoked. A bad artifact or semantic counterexample kills its family pending an explicit protocol revision; do not silently replace a failing mathematical form. Infrastructure failures are recorded and cannot steal budget.
